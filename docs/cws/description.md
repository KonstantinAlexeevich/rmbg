PNG Maker — drop in a photo, get a clean cutout

PNG Maker is a background remover that runs on your device. Add a photo, let the AI find the subject, and save a transparent PNG. No heavy photo editing software, no server queue.

🚀 How to make an image transparent:

1. Install PNG Maker from the Chrome Web Store
2. Click the extension icon to open it
3. Drop in a photo or a batch of images
4. The AI detects the subject and erases the background around it
5. Choose a transparent or solid color backdrop, then download the result

Edge refinement tunes the mask with Threshold, Contract, and Feather when you need a tighter or softer edge.

Everything happens locally. Your photos never leave your browser. Most tools that make a PNG transparent upload first; this PNG image maker never does. After the model download, work stays offline.

❓ Questions and answers

📌 How do I make a PNG transparent?
💡 Add the file and this transparent PNG maker does the rest, removing photo backgrounds on its own. There is no editor to learn: you make a pic transparent in two clicks and download the cutout. It is a clear PNG maker, not a full photo suite.

📌 Will the quality drop?
💡 No. Keep the Original canvas and the cutout matches your source pixel for pixel, so edges stay sharp in print and on screen. It does not upscale or raise photo resolution — nothing is invented, nothing is softened.

📌 Do my pictures get uploaded anywhere?
💡 Never. Your photos stay on the device. The only network step is a one-time download of the on-device AI model weights from Hugging Face. After that the extension works offline, which is also why you can make a photo PNG as often as you like without hitting a daily limit.

📌 Can I make an image a PNG with a colored backdrop instead?
💡 Sure. Switch the background from transparent to any solid color and make clear background PNG files or branded ones from the same shot.

📌 Can I create transparent picture sets for stickers?
💡 Yes. Sticker packs and avatars work the same way, and you can make an image into PNG layers for a video timeline.
